### Purpose

We interact with roadways every day. They connect one place to any other, but they also help define how a city or neighborhood feels.

Is there any sidewalk to speak of?

Perhaps two or three feet of grass between the street and sidewalk?

Maybe trees are dotted along grassy median?

Each of these changes how we feel about the road, and whether we feel the road is meant for cars or for pedestrians.

While we can have an opinion about how nice a road is, we must see it to experience it. Professional roadway designers have the unique challenge of communicating these characteristics of a road that is not yet made. RoadVizVR bridges this communication gap by allowing designers to create roadway designs that lay people can explore in true scale within virtual reality.

### Context

This project was completed as a senior capstone project at Gonzaga University. It is meant to demonstrate the viability of a virtual reality application as a solution to the issue of communicating roadway presence and design.

### Features

#### Lane and Stripe Controls

![image]({{ site.url }}/images/roadvizvr/lanestripecontrols.jpg "Lane and Stripe Edit Menus")
Roads can be designed with a selection of lane types, such as turn lanes and bus lanes, as well as medians, sidewalks, and curbs. The color and patterns of stripes can also be selected.

### Environment Controls

![image]({{ site.url }}/images/roadvizvr/env.jpg "Environment Controls")
The user can control the brightness and amount of fog in the environment, as well as the architecture surrounding the road. The user can select from urban, suburban, and rural settings.

### Props

![image]({{ site.url }}/images/roadvizvr/props.jpg "Prop Placement")
The user can place a variety of items, or "props", around the environment.

### Vehicle Visualization

![image]({{ site.url }}/images/roadvizvr/vehiclecontrols.jpg "Vehicle Movement and Visualization")
The vehicle controls allow users to visualize what it is like for a vehicle to travel down a lane. The sense of presence provided by virtual reality makes this a valuable feature. The lane, vehicle type, and speed can all be selected by the user.

### Saving and Loading

![image]({{ site.url }}/images/roadvizvr/saveload.jpg "Saving and Loading Road Designs")
Saving custom roadway designs allows users to share their ideas.
